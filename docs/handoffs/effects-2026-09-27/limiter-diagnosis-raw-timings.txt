Raw outputs of every timed run behind LIMITER-DIAGNOSIS.md, in the order they were taken.
Each run held the shared timing lock and was pinned to cpu 31; each header line carries the core
clock and /proc/loadavg at that moment. Variant names are the DIAG_VARIANT bits in
limiter-diagnosis-prototypes.patch.

Known invalid rows, kept as taken:
* rows_w8_1.txt, "x:DET": the detector-only truncation was dead-code eliminated (its peaks were
  never read). Fixed with core::hint::black_box from rows_w8_2.txt on.
* rows2_2.txt, "x:DET+DET3": the same elimination for the whole-block scratch. Fixed in rows2_3.txt.
* rows2_3.txt and later: DET3 and TREE numbers come from builds with many instantiated variants;
  final_rows_1.txt, console_rows_final.txt and wasm_console_final_*.txt come from a build that
  instantiated only the final candidates (see section 6 of the diagnosis).
* The "mean" column exists only in unity_rows_1.txt. The bursty and sparse signals give bimodal
  block times, so read their means, not their p50s.
