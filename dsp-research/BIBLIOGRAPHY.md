# Bibliography

All links were selected as bounded primary, official, author-hosted, or publisher sources. Access date: 2026-08-20.

## DSP and measurement

- `[RBJ-COOKBOOK]` Robert Bristow-Johnson, [Audio EQ Cookbook](https://webaudio.github.io/Audio-EQ-Cookbook/audio-eq-cookbook.html), 2022 revision.
- `[REISS-COMP]` D. Giannoulis, M. Massberg, and J. D. Reiss, [Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design—A Tutorial and Analysis](https://eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~josh/documents/2012/GiannoulisMassbergReiss-dynamicrangecompression-JAES2012.pdf), JAES 2012.
- `[ITU-BS1770-5]` ITU-R, [Recommendation BS.1770-5](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I), 2023.
- `[EBU-R128]` EBU, [R 128: Loudness normalisation and permitted maximum level of audio signals](https://tech.ebu.ch/files/live/sites/tech/files/shared/r/r128v5_0.pdf), version 5, 2023.
- `[AES17-PREVIEW]` Audio Engineering Society, [AES17 preview: measurement of digital audio equipment](https://www.aes.org/publications/standards/preview.cfm?ID=21). The full standard is paywalled.
- `[SMITH-SASP]` Julius O. Smith, [Spectral Audio Signal Processing](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/sasp/), online text.
- `[VAIDYANATHAN-MULTIRATE]` P. P. Vaidyanathan, [Multirate Systems and Filter Banks](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/rmhds-22q28), Prentice Hall, 1993.
- `[ORFANIDIS-ISP]` Sophocles J. Orfanidis, *Introduction to Signal Processing*, Prentice Hall, 1996.
- `[DEKKER-EXTENDED]` T. J. Dekker, “A Floating-Point Technique for Extending the Available Precision,” *Numerische Mathematik* 18, 224–242 (1971), [DOI 10.1007/BF01397083](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01397083).
- `[SIMPER-SVF]` Andrew Simper, Cytomic, [Linear Trapezoidal State Variable Filter](https://cytomic.com/files/dsp/SvfLinearTrapezoidalSin.pdf), 2014, revised 2016; author technical derivation and limited-precision coefficient discussion.
- `[ZAVALISHIN-TPT]` Vadim Zavalishin, Native Instruments, [The Art of VA Filter Design](https://www.native-instruments.com/fileadmin/ni_media/downloads/pdf/VAFilterDesign_2.1.0.pdf), revision 2.1.0, 2018; topology-preserving bilinear transform and state-variable-filter derivation.
- `[BILBAO-ADAA]` S. Bilbao, S. Esqueda, J. Parker, and V. Välimäki, [Antiderivative Antialiasing for Memoryless Nonlinearities](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/bitstreams/87839e85-c165-4bd9-aeed-7c96ff64026a/download), IEEE Signal Processing Letters, 2017, DOI [10.1109/LSP.2017.2675541](https://doi.org/10.1109/LSP.2017.2675541).

## Reverberation

Added 2026-10-26 for #2114 (`reverb.md`). DOIs were checked against Crossref. AES e-library pages
are behind a bot check, so their numbers come from the AES search listing and the page itself was
not opened; those entries say so.

- `[JOT-CHAIGNE-FDN]` J.-M. Jot and A. Chaigne, "Digital Delay Networks for Designing Artificial Reverberators," AES 90th Convention, Paris, 1991, preprint 3030, [AES e-library 5663](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=5663) (e-library number from listings; page not opened, paywalled).
- `[STAUTNER-PUCKETTE]` J. Stautner and M. - `[GERZON-UNITARY]` M. A. Gerzon, "Unitary (Energy-Preserving) Multichannel Networks with Feedback," *Electronics Letters* 12(11), 278-279 (1976), [DOI 10.1049/el:19760215](https://doi.org/10.1049/el:19760215).

Habets, "On Lossless Feedback Delay Networks," *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing* 65(6), 1554-1564 (2017), [DOI 10.1109/TSP.2016.2637323](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSP.2016.2637323); [preprint arXiv:1606.07729](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.07729).

- `[SCHLECHT-TIME-VARYING]` S. J. Schlecht and E. A. P. Habets, "Time-Varying Feedback Matrices in Feedback Delay Networks and Their Application in Artificial Reverberation," *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* 138(3), 1389-1398 (2015), [DOI 10.1121/1.4928394](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.4928394).
- `[SCHLECHT-RT60]` S. J. Schlecht and E. A. P. Habets, "Accurate Reverberation Time Control in Feedback Delay Networks," Proc. 20th International Conference on Digital Audio Effects (DAFx-17), Edinburgh, 337-344 (2017), [paper](https://dafx17.eca.ed.ac.uk/papers/DAFx17_paper_11.pdf).
- `[ROCCHESSO-SMITH-CIRCULANT]` D. Rocchesso and J. O. Smith, "Circulant and Elliptic Feedback Delay Networks for Artificial Reverberation," *IEEE Transactions on Speech and Audio Processing* 5(1), 51-63 (1997), [DOI 10.1109/89.554269](https://doi.org/10.1109/89.554269).

- `[SCHROEDER-1962]` M. R. Schroeder, "Natural Sounding Artificial Reverberation," *J. Audio Eng. Soc.* 10(3), 219-223 (1962), [AES e-library 849](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=849) (e-library number from listings; page not opened).

Huang, "A Simple, Robust Measure of Reverberation Echo Density," AES 121st Convention, San Francisco, 2006, preprint 6985, AES e-library 13819 (preprint and e-library numbers from listings; not confirmed on the page); [author copy](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/courses/318/mini-courses/rooms/mus318_Abel_Lecture/echo%20density.pdf).
